# OfflinePay demo video: narration script (Slide 2)

Video is **54.5 s**. Start times below are measured. Speak at about 2.5 words per second, so each line fits its window. If a line runs long, drop its last sentence.
Key point to land: **both phones scan a QR.** The customer scans the merchant's, then the merchant scans the customer's.

| Time | Window | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | 3 s | **Title card** | "Here is the real app, on two phones." |
| 0:03 | 5 s | **Step 1: Load vault**, NPR 1,000 | "The customer is online. They lock NPR 1,000 into a vault on their phone." |
| 0:08 | 3 s | **Step 2: Airplane mode** | "Now both phones go offline. No internet." |
| 0:11 | 4 s | **Step 3: Merchant shows QR**, Rs 150 | "The shop asks for 150 rupees and shows a QR." |
| 0:15 | 4 s | **Step 4: Customer scans the merchant's QR** (*QR scan 1 of 2*, arrow Merchant → Customer) | "Scan one. The customer scans the shop's QR." |

## 20-second version

> "Both phones are offline. The customer scans the shop's QR, then confirms. The phone signs the payment and shows its own QR. The shop scans that, and it's verified on the spot. Edit the amount, and it's rejected. When signal returns, it settles on its own. No signal. No problem."

About 55 words, roughly 20 s. Use it as a closing recap, or as a stand-alone pitch if the video is not playing.
